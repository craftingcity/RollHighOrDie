## CATEGOREY
### Classical Wizardry
#### Force Bolt
Complexity: 12 (4+4+0+4)
Effort: 4 (1+2+0+1)
Speed: 2

**Projectile; Long Range, Immediate, Damage (1d8 Force)**.
Target a creature or point within 90' of you and establish Line of Sight.

Make a Casting Skill Check against your Target's Reflex.

If you hit, you deal 1d8 force damage.

#### Remote Explosion
Complexity: 24 (6+8+5+2+3)
Effort: 18 (4+3+5+2+4)
Speed: 9

**Sight Point; Extreme Range, Sphere (15'), Impose (Flammable), Damage (4d6 Fire)**.
Choose a point within 120', and establish Line of Sight. Compare your Spellcasting roll against each creature within a 15' radius sphere, originating from the point you chose. If you hit, you impose a stack of the 'Flammable' [[Ailment]], then you deal 4d6 fire damage.
